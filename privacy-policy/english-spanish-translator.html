Privacy Policy for English Spanish Translator

Effective Date: May 20, 2026

We operate the English Spanish Translator mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our app.

Information We Collect

We do not collect or store personal data on our servers.

Any translation history, saved phrases, or app preferences are stored locally on your device only and are not transmitted to us.

However, third-party services such as Google Analytics and Google AdMob may collect anonymized information including:

  • Device information
  • App activity and usage statistics
  • Advertising identifiers

Permissions Used

Our app may use the following permissions to provide core functionality:

  • INTERNET – Used to access online translation services, analytics, and advertisements.
  • RECORD_AUDIO – Used only when the user chooses voice input or speech translation features within the app.
  • VIBRATE – Used to provide vibration feedback for certain user interactions and notifications.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED – Used to restore app-related notifications or scheduled features after the device restarts.

File Access and Processing

Our app accesses PDF, Word, and other supported files only when explicitly selected by the user through Android’s secure system file picker. The selected files are used solely for translation and processing purposes within the app. We do not scan, collect, or access unrelated files stored on your device, and no file data is shared with third parties.

How We Use Information

  • Improve app performance and stability
  • Analyze usage trends
  • Display advertisements
  • Enhance user experience

Data Retention

We do not retain personal data on external servers.

All user data stored by the app remains on your device until:

  • You manually delete the data
  • You clear the app storage
  • You uninstall the application

Third-party services may retain anonymized data according to their own privacy policies.

Data Deletion

You have full control over your data.

You can delete app data at any time by:

  • Clearing app data from your app history
  • Uninstalling the app

We do not have access to your locally stored data and cannot delete it remotely.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to collect anonymized usage data that helps us improve app functionality and user experience.

Advertising

Our app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect anonymized data to provide personalized and relevant ads.

Translation Features

  • English to Spanish Translation
  • Spanish to English Translation
  • PDF & WORD document Translation
  • Voice Translation
  • Saved Translation History

Children's Privacy

This app is intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age.

Third-Party Services
